My Principles

  1. Web designing should show your purpose.
  2. Experiment with all the tools you've been given.
  3. Making mistakes will always be a way for a learning opportunity.

Why I want to learn Web Design

I am learning web design because I want to understand how websites can be made in creative ways. I want to experiment with new ideas I am thinking of along the way, and creating something that I find meaningful to me the most.
